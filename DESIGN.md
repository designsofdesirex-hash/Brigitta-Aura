# Brigitta Aura visual system

## Direction

An image-led, quiet editorial for a relaxed feet content creator. The design earns its identity through Brigitta's supplied vertical video, a distinctive plum-and-cream atmosphere, elegant type proportions, and a direct Instagram path. It contains no booking funnel or generic marketplace language.

## Palette and type

Deep plum `#24151d` is the main evening surface; cream `#f3ebdf` creates the quiet editorial pause; coral `#ef765f` owns the proof field; peach `#ffb36b` marks action; periwinkle `#7f8dff` is a secondary action. Bodoni Moda is the display face; Plus Jakarta Sans handles all navigation, body copy, and controls.

## Composition

The first screen is an oversized promise paired with one tall video crop. The manifesto is a single generous text field. Services are three large open rows, not cards. Media is a video-led colour break. Contact is one direct Instagram close with PayPal clearly marked soon.

## Interaction and responsive rules

Buttons are substantial, full-width on small screens, and respond with a short lift and press scale. Image hover uses a restrained settle. Reduced motion removes transforms. The desktop hero is two columns; mobile becomes a clean vertical reading path with no decorative side rails.

The contact close has no form or booking system. It uses one active Instagram action and two non-interactive coming-soon states.

## Content boundaries

English is the only published language. The active route is Instagram `brigittaaura`; PayPal remains a placeholder marked Soon. The supplied Brigitta Aura video is used. No prices, testimonials, payment handles, or unsupported claims are fabricated.
