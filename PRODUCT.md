## Platform

## Stack

## Users

Adults 18+ seeking relaxed, consensual feet content and personal creative sessions with Brigitta Aura.

## Product Purpose

An official, bilingual web presence for Brigitta Aura that sets the atmosphere, presents online sessions and custom media, and routes enquiries to Instagram.

## Positioning

Brigitta Aura is a relaxed feet content creator whose work is defined by attention to details, colours, texture, light, and mood.

## Operating Context

Visitors confirm they are adults, experience the visual mood, choose an online session or custom photos/videos, then contact Brigitta through Instagram.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- English is the only published language.
- Services include online sessions, custom photos, and custom videos.
- Instagram is the active contact route at https://www.instagram.com/brigittaaura/; PayPal is shown as coming soon.
- The site must include an 18+ gate, responsive behavior, accessible controls, and a clear media preview.
- Pricing, payment handles, testimonials, and availability must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

Use Brigitta Aura as the client name. The voice is relaxed, intimate, observant, and grounded in details, colours, texture, mood, and personal attention. Keep adult content clearly marked and consensual in framing.

## Product Principles

- Authenticity before theatre.
- Direct contact over cluttered funnels.
- Let Brigitta's own voice lead.
- Make the offer legible in seconds.
- Treat adult visitors with clear boundaries and respect.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The page must retain keyboard focus visibility, readable contrast, reduced-motion behavior, labelled media controls, and a usable bilingual switch on narrow screens.
